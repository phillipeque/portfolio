Hey, I'm Phillipe — community builder, manager, and moderator who genuinely does this out of passion, not just a job title. I've spent my time building communities from scratch and stepping into existing ones to rebuild what wasn't working, figuring out what's broken before it becomes a bigger problem.

I like digging into the details other people skip — spotting gaps, fixing dynamics, making spaces actually feel good to be in. If it involves people, structure, and a little bit of chaos to organize, that's where I do my best work.

My experience spans Web3, fintech, SaaS, gaming, and community-driven products.
