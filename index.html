_ Hey, I'm Phillipe — a Brazilian community builder, manager, and moderator driven by passion. Beyond managing people and dynamics, I specialize in developing custom tools and automation to streamline operations, optimize workflows, and enhance community engagement — including building tailored bots for Discord and Telegram. With over 6 years of experience across more than 20 communities, I've learned that every space has its own rhythm.

I've built communities from scratch and stepped into existing ones to identify gaps, fix broken systems, and keep everything running seamlessly. I thrive on digging into details, building the right infrastructure, and turning chaotic spaces into well-structured, engaging environments.

My approach blends strategy with empathy — I don't just enforce rules or set up channels. I listen, adapt, and create systems that actually work for the people inside them. When I'm not architecting communities, I'm probably experimenting with new automation tools, contributing to open-source projects, or thinking about how to make the next community even better.
